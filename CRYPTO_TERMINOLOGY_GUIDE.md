# Crypto Terminology & Concepts Guide

1. **RPC**: Bridge that connects mobile app to blockchain nodes.
2. **Sepolia Testnet**: Ethereum testing network for safe development.
3. **On-Ramp**: Buying crypto with fiat (INR/USD via UPI or Card).
4. **Off-Ramp**: Selling crypto back to fiat (bank account payout).
5. **Non-Custodial**: User holds full ownership and control of their keys.
